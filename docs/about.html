Dartmouth's pizza shop for over 20 years

Tasty Pizza is on 760 Main St. in Dartmouth, just off the #7 hwy. Owned and operated by the Patel family.

The dough is made fresh every morning and stretched by hand, and the pizzas go into a deck oven. Garlic fingers come with donair sauce, and the donairs are the Halifax kind, carved off the spit.

We are open seven days, till 1am on Friday, and we deliver to Cole Harbour, Forest Hill, Lake Echo, Westphal, Woodlawn, Colby, Lawrencetown, Eastern Passage, Mineville.

Questions

What are your hours?

Sun 2pm–10pm; Mon–Thu 11am–11pm; Fri 11am–1am; Sat 11am–12am. Hours can change on holidays, so call 902-435-5700 if you are unsure.

Do you deliver?

Yes, to Cole Harbour, Forest Hill, Lake Echo, Westphal, Woodlawn, Colby, Lawrencetown, Eastern Passage and Mineville. Delivery on orders over $10 after 2pm in our local area. The delivery charge is $2.00.

Where are you?

760 Main St. in Dartmouth, just off the #7 hwy. Parking is free.

How do I order?

Put your order together on this site, then call it in on 902-435-5700 or order through DoorDash or Uber Eats.

How does build your own work?

Pick a size and tap toppings — a 12" Medium with cheese is $13.95, and 5 toppings makes it a Tasty Pizza Special. You can add more past that; the price of each extra one shows as you go.

Do you have gluten-free crust?

Yes, gluten-free crust is +$6.45. White crust costs nothing extra.

What vegetarian pizzas do you have?

Greek, Cheese Lovers and Vegetarian, plus any pizza you build yourself.

How long does pickup take?

About 20–30 min.

How can I pay?

Visa and Mastercard, and debit at the door on request. Prices do not include HST.
